## Files to submit

1. Enter the two lists in the Unstop rank dropdowns, ranks 1 through 10.
2. Upload `pressure_performance_methodology.pdf` as the methodology document. It contains the pressure rules, batting and bowling metrics, sample gates, scoring formulas, both evidence tables, reliability checks, limitations, and reproduction notes.
3. Keep `batter_top10.csv` and `bowler_top10.csv` as supporting ranked-list files if the round accepts optional attachments. The Unstop fields are the authoritative selections.

The list covers eligible players across the supplied IPL and T20I data. Rajasthan Royals colors are used for the project's presentation; the leaderboard is not restricted to RR squad members.

## One-minute explanation

“Pressure Playbook tags each delivery using the match state before the ball. It compares a batter's pressure strike and dismissal rates, or a bowler's pressure economy and wicket rates, with that same player's ordinary baseline. Minimum ball and match thresholds screen low-exposure samples, and a shrinkage factor tempers the scores when pressure exposure is small. The result is a transparent ranking for the supplied IPL and T20I dataset, with player-level evidence and stated limits rather than a claim to predict match outcomes.”
